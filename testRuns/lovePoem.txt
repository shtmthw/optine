Love is a quiet flame that glows
In the silence of a crowded room.
It whispers in the hush of twilight,
And paints the stars with gentle hues.
It walks beside us through seasons,
A gentle hand that steadies hearts.
In laughter and in silence, it sings,
A rhythm that no time can part.
It is a promise, a gentle truth,
A dance of souls that never ends.
In each beat, a promise made,
Love's song forever in our hands.
